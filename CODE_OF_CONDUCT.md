# FloatPlay Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

FloatPlay contributors and maintainers are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We commit to acting and interacting in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting differing viewpoints and experiences;
- giving and receiving constructive feedback professionally;
- accepting responsibility and apologizing when mistakes affect others;
- focusing on what is best for the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention;
- publishing another person's private information without explicit permission;
- sustained disruption of project discussions or collaboration;
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all FloatPlay project spaces, including issues, pull requests, code review, repository discussions, and other public or private project communication. It also applies when someone is officially representing FloatPlay in public spaces.

## Reporting and enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior and may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

If a conduct concern can be handled publicly, report it to the maintainer through the repository's normal issue or contribution workflow. If public disclosure would be inappropriate, contact the maintainer through a private contact method available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct reports and must follow `.github/SECURITY.md` and GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting instead of a public issue.

Reports will be reviewed with reasonable discretion and respect for the privacy and safety of the people involved. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Enforcement guidelines

Maintainers may apply responses proportionate to the impact and context of a violation, including:

1. **Correction** — a private or public clarification and request to change behavior.
2. **Warning** — a formal warning for inappropriate behavior or a pattern of violations.
3. **Temporary restriction** — temporary limits on participation in project spaces.
4. **Permanent restriction** — removal from project participation for sustained, severe, or repeated violations.
