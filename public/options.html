FloatPlay Settings

Choose how the mini player controls behave while you watch.

On YouTube, click the FloatPlay icon beside the subscription and notification controls to open the mini player.

Playback controls

Set how far the video moves and how much the volume changes with each action.

seconds

Used when you go back from the player or press Left Arrow.

seconds

Used when you go forward from the player or press Right Arrow.

%

How much the volume changes at a time with the wheel or Up/Down Arrow.

Control visibility

Choose whether controls get out of the way while the video is playing.

seconds

Time without interaction before the controls disappear.

Keyboard shortcuts

Available while the mini player is focused.

Space
Play / Pause
← →
Go back / Go forward
↑ ↓
Volume up / down
M
Mute / Unmute
[ ]
Playback speed down / up
